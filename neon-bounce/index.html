About the game

Aim, shoot and watch hundreds of balls ricochet across a glowing neon board. Every hit is a note: chain hits in a single shot and the bricks play a melody that rises with your combo.

Every 10 levels you pick one of three upgrades — extra balls, more power, lasers, a breather or a purge of the bottom row — so no two runs feel the same. Every 25 levels a giant boss brick drops onto the board. Take it down for diamonds.

How to play

  1. DragTouch the screen and drag to aim.
  2. ReleaseLet go to fire a stream of balls.
  3. SurviveBreak the bricks before they reach the bottom.
