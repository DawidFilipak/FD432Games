About the game

Block Down is a physics puzzle game about one simple goal: knock every block off the platform. Every level is a small puzzle — find the weak spot, use as few cannonballs as possible and earn all three stars.

Light the TNT for huge chain reactions, pop balloons, topple dominoes and shatter glass and ice. Levels are short, so it's perfect for a quick break — and it works offline. Can you get three stars on every level?

How to play

  1. AimDrag anywhere — the dotted line shows your shot.
  2. FireTap FIRE to launch the cannonball.
  3. Clear the platformKnock all the blocks off before you run out of shots.
