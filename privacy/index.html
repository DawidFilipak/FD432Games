3. What we don't collect

Our games have no user accounts. They don't ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or files. We don't run our own servers for the games, and we don't sell your data.

4. Data stored only on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, diamonds), unlocked items, settings and the information that you bought an option such as “Remove ads” are stored locally on your phone. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings. If Android backup is turned on, Google may include this data in your device backup in your Google account.

5. Google services that process data

5.1 Advertising: Google AdMob

Our games are free and show ads (banners, full-screen ads and optional rewarded ads) through Google AdMob. To show ads and measure them, Google processes your device's advertising ID, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device information (such as model, operating system and language) and your interactions with ads (views and clicks).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form the first time you open it. Personalised ads are shown only if you agree. Without consent you see non-personalised ads, which are based on context. Google still uses limited data to deliver them, to limit how often you see the same ad, to prevent fraud and to produce aggregated reports.

The list of Google's advertising partners is available in the consent form in the game. More information: How Google uses information in advertising.

5.2 Usage statistics: Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played: for example which levels are too hard, where players stop and which upgrades and missions are used. This involves game events, an app instance identifier, device model, operating system version, language, approximate country and session length. The events depend on the game:

  • Neon Bounce: level started, completed or failed, upgrade chosen, mission completed, purchase made.
  • Block Down: level start and end (result, stars, number of shots, time), in-game coins earned and spent, special balls used, ads shown, tutorial progress, daily reward, endless mode and daily challenge.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics storage is turned off by default and is turned on only if you consent in the consent form. Without consent, Analytics runs in Google's restricted consent mode, without analytics identifiers.

5.3 Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics

If the game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a report: the error log and stack trace, a Crashlytics installation identifier, device model, operating system version and the state of the device at the time of the crash (for example free memory and screen orientation). We use these reports only to find and fix bugs.

5.4 In-app purchases: Google Play

Some games let you buy an option such as “Remove ads”. Currently this applies only to Neon Bounce; Block Down has no payments. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the game uses Unity In-App Purchasing to communicate with Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The game receives only a confirmation of which product was bought and a purchase token, so it can unlock the product and restore it after you reinstall the game.

5.5 Local notifications

Some games can show reminders, for example about a daily reward. These reminders are scheduled on your phone; no server is involved and no data is sent. You can turn them off in Android's notification settings.

7. Recipients and transfers outside the EU

Data is processed by Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC, USA) as the provider of AdMob, Firebase and Google Play. For advertising, data is also processed by Google's certified advertising partners listed in the consent form. Their privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Privacy and security in Firebase
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Unity Privacy Policy (in-app purchase library)

Some data is transferred to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and Google also uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

8. How long data is kept

  • Usage statistics: 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: about 90 days.
  • Advertising data: according to Google's retention policy.
  • Data on your device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Messages you send us: only as long as needed to handle your request.

9. Changing your consent

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that took place before.

  • In the game: open the privacy settings to show the consent form again.
    • Neon Bounce: Settings → Privacy → Consent settings
    • Block Down: the PRIVACY button in the main menu (shown only to players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland); the PRIVACY POLICY button in the main menu opens this page
  • On Android: go to Settings → Google → All services → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device) and reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Uninstalling the game stops all processing by the game and deletes its local data.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it,
  • have your data corrected,
  • have your data deleted,
  • restrict processing,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • data portability,
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

To use these rights, e-mail fd432games@gmail.com. Because the games have no accounts, we don't know who you are. To find data from your device, we may ask for the name of the game and the approximate time you played it. We will never ask for passwords.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl). You can also contact the authority in your own country.

11. Deleting your data

  • Data on your device: uninstall the game, or go to Settings → Apps → the game → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Statistics and crash reports: deleted automatically after the periods in section 8. To ask for earlier deletion, e-mail fd432games@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion” and the name of the game. We'll handle the request within one month.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID (section 9) and manage ad settings in Google My Ad Center.

12. Children

Our games are intended for players aged 13 and over and are not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used one of our games and their data was processed, contact us and we will help to delete it.

13. This website

fd432games.com doesn't use cookies, analytics or ads, and game trailers are played directly from this site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site and keep it secure; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we release a new game or start using a new service. The date at the top shows the current version. We'll describe significant changes on this page and, where required, ask for your consent again in the game.
